About

Built for the way departments actually schedule

Arranging cover for an absent faculty member is routine work that quietly consumes a department's time. TecSubstitution keeps every timetable in one normalized form, so the question "who is free at this period" has one answer.

One master timetable

Class timetables and individual faculty weeks are two views of the same validated dataset — never two copies that drift apart.

Answers, not spreadsheets

Finding cover by hand means cross-reading every other faculty timetable at the same coordinate. One click does it here.

Read-only by design

Checking availability never assigns anyone or edits a timetable. The only operation that changes data is an import you explicitly confirm.

Features

Everything the scheduling desk needs

Each feature below is implemented and covered by the automated test suite.

Centralized Master Timetable

View any class timetable or a single faculty member's own week, with period timings and room allocation.

1-Click Substitute Finding

Click the period an absent colleague was teaching and every free faculty member at that exact coordinate is listed.

Clickable Timetable Grid

Every cell is a real control carrying its own day, period, class, subject, faculty and room.

Excel & CSV Import

How It Works

From a paper timetable to a substitute list

Five steps, in the order the dashboard walks you through them.

  1. 01

    Upload Timetable

    Bring in an Excel or CSV timetable, paste one from a printed sheet, or start from a department preset.

  2. 02

    Extract Timetable Data

    The importer reads the sheet, detects whether it is a matrix or long-form layout, and pulls out every slot.

  3. 03

    Validate Information

    Days, periods, duplicates and double-bookings are checked. Errors block the load; warnings are shown, never hidden.

  4. 04

    Find Substitute Faculty

    Click the period that needs cover. Every faculty member free at that exact day and period is listed, with the busy ones and their reason.

  5. 05

    Review and Manage Timetable

    Review workload, conflicts and the week's availability summary. The result is informational — nothing is assigned automatically.
