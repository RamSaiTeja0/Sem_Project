# TecSubstitution: Final Project Demonstration Flow & Presentation Guide

---

## 1. DEMO OBJECTIVE

The primary objective of this presentation is to demonstrate that **TecSubstitution** is a fully functional, conflict-free, and branch-isolated academic scheduling platform that solves the institutional challenge of faculty absence and timetable substitution.

The demonstration proves that:
1. **Master & Individual Timetables** are digitally managed without schedule clashes or double-booking.
2. **Faculty Absences** directly and dynamically update the system's availability state.
3. **Faculty Availability** is calculated in real time, screening out teaching sessions, absences, and exam duties while prioritizing same-branch candidates.
4. **Peer-to-Peer Faculty Substitution (Option A)** operates seamlessly between instructors with atomic availability revalidation, while keeping master timetables untouched and giving the Head of Section (HOS) read-only oversight.
5. **Examination Invigilation** is tracked independently so that exam supervision duties make instructors busy without falsely marking them absent.
6. **Multi-Tenant Branch Isolation** prevents unauthorized cross-departmental data tampering.
7. **Cloud Persistence (Neon PostgreSQL)** guarantees that all records survive server restarts.
8. **AI-Assisted Timetable Ingestion** securely extracts schedules via Google Gemini Vision and n8n into staging tables with mandatory human HOS review before live import.

> [!IMPORTANT]

   - **Window 1 (Normal Window)**: Logged in as **Head of Section (HOS)**.

   - Keep only the project dashboard, the project report, and the terminal open.
   - Pre-scale the browser zoom to 100% or 110% for optimal projection legibility.

## 5. DEMO SCRIPT (STEP-BY-STEP)

### Step A: HOS Login & Dashboard Overview
- **Screen / Action**: Open `http://localhost:3000/login`. Sign in with HOS credentials. The system redirects to `http://localhost:3000/dashboard`.
- **What to Do**: Point to the top bar showing active Branch badge (e.g., `CME`) and user role (`Head of Section`). Point to the metric cards (Total Faculty, Classes, Timetable Entries).
- **What to Say**:
  > *"Good morning, respected evaluators. Today we present TecSubstitution, an institutional platform designed to solve the critical problem of faculty absence and timetable substitution. We begin signed in as the Head of Section for Computer Engineering. The top bar confirms our authenticated department context. Every administrative action taken in this session is automatically scoped to this department."*

### Step B: Faculty Management
- **Screen / Action**: Click **Faculty Management** (`#navFaculty`) in the sidebar.
- **What to Do**: Display the faculty roster table. Highlight faculty names, designations, phone numbers, and max weekly teaching periods.
- **What to Say**:
  > *"Under Faculty Management, the department head maintains the teaching staff directory. When an HOS registers a faculty member, that account automatically inherits the department's branch code. Faculty members cannot modify or transfer their branch affiliation, preserving institutional security."*

### Step C: Master Timetable & Class Schedules
- **Screen / Action**: Click **Master Timetable** (`#navTimetable`) in the sidebar.
- **What to Do**: Select a class section (e.g., `CME-A`) and Semester. Point to the weekly grid displaying Monday through Saturday and Periods 1 through 7. Point out a laboratory block spanning multiple periods.
- **What to Say**:
  > *"Here is the Master Class Timetable. The grid renders weekly schedules from Period 1 to Period 7. Notice how laboratory practicals visually span consecutive periods with distinct session badges. The system enforces strict conflict prevention: if an HOS attempts to schedule a teacher or classroom for two simultaneous classes, the application and database immediately reject the duplicate booking."*

### Step D: Faculty Absence Logging
- **Screen / Action**: Click **Faculty Attendance** (`#navAttendance`) in the sidebar.
- **What to Do**: Select today's date (or target demo date). Locate **Faculty A**. Click **Mark Absent**. Enter leave reason *"Official University Duty"*. Save.
- **What to Say**:
  > *"Now let us observe the operational workflow. In any institution, teachers are presumed PRESENT by default. However, when Faculty A takes leave today, the HOS records their absence with a single click. This absence is date-specific and does not corrupt Faculty A's recurring weekly timetable, but it immediately updates the availability engine."*

### Step E: Availability Evaluation
- **Screen / Action**: Click **Faculty Availability** (`#navAvailability`) in the sidebar.
- **What to Do**: Select the day and period corresponding to Faculty A's scheduled lecture (e.g., Friday, Period 3). Set date to the demo date. Click **Check Availability**.
- **What to Say**:
  > *"When we query availability for Friday Period 3, the engine evaluates multi-dimensional institutional data. Notice that Faculty A is marked BUSY because they are on leave. Other instructors who are currently teaching classes or supervising exams are also marked BUSY. Free colleagues are prioritized: teachers in our department appear under 'Priority Branch', followed by eligible teachers from allied branches under 'Other Branches'."*

### Step F: Peer-to-Peer Substitution (Core Feature)
- **Screen / Action**: Switch to the **Faculty A** browser window (or navigate to **Faculty Substitutions** `#navFacultySubstitutions`).
- **What to Do**:
  1. As Faculty A, view the vacant teaching period.
  2. Click **Request Substitute**.
  3. Select free colleague **Faculty B** from the list. Submit the request.
  4. Switch to **Faculty B's** view. Point to the badge counter on `#navFacultySubstitutions` indicating an incoming request.
  5. Open the incoming request modal showing Class, Subject, Room, Date, and Period.
  6. Click **Accept**. Show the real-time confirmation.
- **What to Say**:
  > *"This is the core feature of our project: the Peer-to-Peer Faculty Substitution workflow (Option A). Faculty A identifies their vacant period and selects free colleague Faculty B. When Faculty B logs in, an incoming notification card appears. When Faculty B clicks 'Accept', our backend performs an atomic revalidation: it confirms Faculty B is still free before committing. Once accepted, Faculty B is marked BUSY for that slot, and the substitution is logged. Crucially, the HOS does not need to manually approve or intervene, avoiding morning delays, and the original master timetable remains 100% untouched."*

### Step G: Exam Invigilation Management
- **Screen / Action**: Switch back to the HOS window. Click **Exam Invigilation** (`#navInvigilation`).
- **What to Do**: Display the invigilation schedule. Show an instructor assigned to an examination hall for Period 4.
- **What to Say**:
  > *"TecSubstitution also manages Examination Invigilation. When an HOS assigns an instructor to exam supervision duty, the system marks them BUSY for that specific period. This ensures that invigilating teachers are never mistakenly approached for class substitutions, while recognizing that they are present on campus."*

### Step H: AI Timetable Extraction (Staging & Approval)
- **Screen / Action**: Click **Upload Timetable** (`#navImport`) or **Staged Review** in Master Timetable.
- **What to Do**: Display the staged preview grid extracted from `sample_timetable.jpeg`. Point to the `VALID` badge, the extracted classes, and the entity resolution panel.
- **What to Say**:
  > *"To modernize timetable ingestion, we integrated an AI extraction bridge using Google Gemini Vision and n8n. Scanned timetable images or PDFs are parsed into strict JSON contracts and placed in an isolated staging table. Crucially, the AI model never writes directly to live academic records. The HOS must review the staged schedule, resolve any uncatalogued teacher names, and explicitly click 'Approve' to execute an atomic live import."*

### Step I: Multi-Tenant Branch Isolation
- **Screen / Action**: Open developer tools or demonstrate via an API call/second department login (e.g., EEE).
- **What to Do**: Show that an HOS of CME cannot access or view EEE faculty rosters or class entries, receiving `HTTP 403 Forbidden`.
- **What to Say**:
  > *"Multi-tenant security is enforced throughout. Every database query is parameterized with the session's department code. A department head in Computer Engineering cannot alter or view Civil or Electrical Engineering records."*

### Step J: Database Persistence Verification
- **Screen / Action**: Open `http://localhost:3000/api/storage` in a clean tab.
- **What to Do**: Point to `backend: "postgres"`, showing that the system is backed by remote PostgreSQL (Neon).
- **What to Say**:
  > *"Finally, all system data—including master timetables, attendance, invigilations, and substitutions—is durably stored in PostgreSQL on Neon serverless. In our formal persistence test suite, 10 distinct institutional entity types were created, the server was shut down and restarted, and all records were 100% restored without data loss."*

## 10. FAILURE RECOVERY PLAN

| Potential Demonstration Issue | Immediate Fallback Action | What to Explain to Reviewers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cloud Database Latency / Disconnection** | Restart server locally; fallback to pre-loaded demo cache if network drops. | *"The platform includes an automated fallback to in-memory datasets if cloud database connectivity drops, ensuring uninterrupted institutional access."* |
| **Gemini AI API Key Limit / Network Drop** | Do not perform a live external API call. Navigate directly to the pre-staged timetable review panel. | *"Rather than waiting for external multimodal API network latency, here is the pre-staged extraction result showing the exact contract validation and HOS approval screen."* |
| **Candidate Already Booked during Demo** | Pick another candidate or select a different period (e.g., Period 4 instead of Period 3). | *"The system correctly prevented double-booking because that colleague had another obligation. Let us choose a colleague who is genuinely free."* |
| **Accidental Session Expiration** | Quickly sign back in using the pre-filled browser credentials. | *"Our session cookies are cryptographically signed with HMAC-SHA256 and expire automatically for institutional security."* |

## 11. REVIEWER-FRIENDLY PROJECT STORY

When presenting the motivation and flow, structure your narrative using this logical progression:

1. **The Problem**: *"In technical colleges, morning faculty absences create sudden classroom vacancies. Manual substitution via phone calls or notes is chaotic, slow, and causes double-booking."*
2. **The Foundation**: *"TecSubstitution digitizes master timetables with multi-period lab support and mathematical conflict prevention."*
3. **The Trigger**: *"When an instructor is absent, the HOS records it in seconds, updating daily attendance."*
4. **The Intelligence**: *"The availability engine instantly screens out busy, absent, and invigilating teachers, ranking free colleagues."*
5. **The Innovation**: *"Instructors arrange coverage directly via peer substitution (Option A) with atomic revalidation, leaving the master schedule intact."*
6. **The Integration**: *"Exam duties and AI document extraction are integrated into a secure, multi-tenant PostgreSQL database."*
