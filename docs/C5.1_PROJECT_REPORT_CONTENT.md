# TecSubstitution: Academic Project Report Content

---

## 1. ABSTRACT

In modern polytechnic and collegiate engineering institutions, academic continuity depends heavily on adherence to weekly class timetables. However, unexpected faculty absences, official leaves, and scheduled examination invigilation duties regularly disrupt lecture delivery. Traditional manual processes for tracking faculty availability, locating substitute teachers, and handling class substitutions are slow, error-prone, and prone to scheduling conflicts such as double-booking. 

**TecSubstitution** is a centralized, web-based timetable management and faculty substitution platform designed specifically for institutional academic departments. The system provides an end-to-end digital solution encompassing institutional timetable maintenance, real-time faculty availability analysis, daily attendance/absence logging, examination invigilation assignment, and a peer-to-peer faculty substitution workflow. 

TecSubstitution enforces strict departmental and branch isolation, ensuring that each Head of Section (HOS) administers only their branch's academic schedule, faculty roster, and attendance records, while allowing cross-departmental visibility for discovering available substitution candidates when local faculty are occupied. To modernize schedule ingestion, the platform incorporates an AI-assisted timetable extraction pipeline using Google Gemini multimodal vision integrated with an n8n workflow bridge. This extraction pipeline safely parses scanned image and PDF timetables into structured JSON contracts and stages them in dedicated staging tables. Crucially, the AI pipeline does not perform automatic substitute assignment nor does it write directly to live academic records; all staged schedules require explicit HOS review, entity resolution, and transactional approval. Built on Node.js, Express, Vanilla CSS, and a serverless Neon PostgreSQL relational database, the system eliminates paper dependency, prevents double-booking at both application and database constraint levels, and maintains complete auditability across all academic operations.

## 2. INTRODUCTION

Academic scheduling in technical institutions is a complex operational task. A single academic department typically operates multiple classes, laboratories, and specialized workshop sessions across different semesters and sections every weekday. Each teaching slot involves a designated class section, classroom or laboratory venue, assigned subject curriculum, and a designated faculty instructor.

When a faculty member is absent due to illness, emergency leave, or institutional duty (such as university exam invigilation), their scheduled classes must either be cancelled or covered by an available colleague. In conventional institutional environments, this substitution process is managed informally through telephone calls, handwritten notes, or corridor discussions. This lack of centralized visibility frequently leads to:
1. Class slots being left unmonitored or unhandled.
2. Colleagues being asked to cover classes when they already have scheduled teaching or invigilation duties (double-booking).
3. Inequitable workload distribution among faculty members.
4. Absence of reliable records for department heads to verify whether missed lectures were covered.

TecSubstitution was developed to address these operational bottlenecks. By establishing a centralized digital representation of master timetables, real-time availability calculations, daily attendance statuses, and active invigilation assignments, the application gives faculty and administration clear, conflict-free visibility over campus teaching schedules.

## 3. PROBLEM STATEMENT

Manual and ad-hoc management of faculty timetables and substitution arrangements exhibits critical operational deficiencies:

1. **Information Asymmetry and Delays**: When a faculty member announces an unscheduled absence in the morning, finding a free colleague for period 1 or period 2 requires consulting paper timetable charts posted on departmental notice boards. This takes excessive time and often results in lost instructional hours.
2. **Double-Booking and Scheduling Clashes**: Manual substitution arrangements cannot reliably cross-reference whether a nominated substitute already has a class in another semester, an existing laboratory session, an off-campus duty, or an assigned examination invigilation slot during that exact period.
3. **Absence of Invigilation and Attendance Integration**: In many institutions, examination invigilation rosters and daily staff attendance registers exist as isolated paper documents. A teacher marked absent in the attendance register might still be approached for substitution, or a teacher on exam duty may be mistakenly assumed to be free for classroom teaching.
4. **Tedious Timetable Digitization**: At the beginning of every semester, institutional timetables are generated or printed as complex visual matrices (spreadsheets or PDF/scanned documents). Manually re-typing dozens of multi-period lab blocks, section assignments, and teacher names into a database is tedious and prone to transcription errors.
5. **Lack of Departmental Boundaries**: In multi-branch institutions (e.g., Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Civil Engineering), administrative data must be securely isolated so that one department head cannot inadvertently alter or overwrite another department's faculty records or class entries.

## 4. OBJECTIVES

The core objectives of the TecSubstitution system are:

1. **Digital Timetable Management**: Provide an interactive weekly grid interface (Periods 1 to 7, Monday through Saturday) to view, add, edit, and organize class timetables by branch, academic year, semester, and section.
2. **Faculty Timetable Views**: Enable every faculty member to inspect their personalized weekly teaching schedule with dedicated room numbers and subject codes.
3. **Departmental & Branch Isolation**: Enforce multi-tenant administrative boundaries where Heads of Section (HOS) have exclusive administrative control over their own department's roster, classes, and timetables.
4. **Faculty Attendance and Absence Tracking**: Enable the HOS to record daily staff attendance (Present/Absent status with remarks), automatically linking absence data to scheduling logic.
5. **Real-Time Faculty Availability Engine**: Provide a read-only availability calculation engine that cross-references class timetables, daily absence logs, and invigilation duties to identify genuinely free faculty candidates for any given day and period.
6. **Examination Invigilation Management**: Allow HOS administrators to assign and schedule faculty to examination halls, while allowing faculty to submit invigilation requests.
7. **Peer-to-Peer Faculty Substitution**: Implement a structured workflow allowing an absent faculty member to select a free colleague, send a substitution request, and allow the colleague to accept or decline the request.
8. **Double-Booking & Conflict Prevention**: Ensure that no faculty member or classroom venue can be double-booked for the same time slot, enforced through both business validation algorithms and relational PostgreSQL database constraints.
9. **Role-Based Authentication & Authorization**: Provide secure session-based authentication with strict role segregation between institutional Heads of Section (HOS) and teaching Faculty.
10. **Persistent Relational Storage**: Ensure that all institution data (branches, classes, faculty, timetables, attendance, invigilations, substitutions) persists safely in a cloud-hosted Neon PostgreSQL database.
11. **AI-Assisted Timetable Ingestion & Approval Gateway**: Support automated extraction of visual timetables (PDFs and images) into structured JSON formats via Google Gemini vision and n8n webhook integration, staging the output for mandatory human HOS review and approval prior to live insertion.

## 5. EXISTING SYSTEM

In a traditional or manual institutional system:

- **Medium**: Timetables are prepared using desktop word processors or spreadsheets, printed on paper, and pinned to staff room bulletin boards.
- **Substitution Procedure**: When a teacher is absent, the teacher or section in-charge physically walks around the department or calls colleagues individually to check if anyone is free during the vacant period.
- **Record Keeping**: Substitution arrangements are noted down in a physical register or left unrecorded.
- **Coordination with Exams**: Examination invigilation duties are printed on separate circular notices and are not cross-referenced with daily timetable movements.

### Limitations of the Existing System:
- High consumption of administrative time during peak morning hours.
- Human error leading to double-booked teachers and unmanned classrooms.
- Inability to check availability of teachers from other allied departments when local departmental staff are fully occupied.
- Lack of institutional accountability, historical auditing, or reporting of substitution patterns.
- High barrier to updating schedules when class rooms or faculty allocations change mid-semester.

## 6. PROPOSED SYSTEM

TecSubstitution replaces disparate paper records and manual coordination with a unified, browser-accessible platform accessible to administrators and teaching staff.

### System Roles and Permissions

The system operates with two primary authenticated roles:

#### 1. Head of Section (HOS)
- **Scope**: Department Administrator (e.g., HOS of Computer Engineering).
- **Capabilities**:
  - Register and manage their departmental profile (Branch Code, Department Name, Academic Year, Total Semesters).
  - Create and manage faculty profiles within their branch (Faculty Name, Designation, Email, Max Weekly Periods, System Password).
  - Create and manage class sections (e.g., CME-A, CME-B) and subject catalogs (Theory, Lab, Activity).
  - Add, edit, or delete live class timetable entries across all semesters in their branch.
  - Mark and maintain daily faculty attendance records (Present / Absent / Leave).
  - Assign exam invigilation duties directly or approve/reject faculty-submitted invigilation requests.
  - Upload PDF/image timetables for AI extraction, inspect the staged extraction matrix, map unresolved faculty/subjects, and grant final transactional import approval.
  - View a read-only audit log of all substitution requests and completions within their branch.
- **Restrictions**:
  - Cannot create, alter, or delete timetable entries, faculty profiles, or attendance in another department (enforced with HTTP 403 Forbidden).
  - Cannot initiate or accept faculty-to-faculty substitution requests on behalf of instructors.

#### 2. Faculty Member
- **Scope**: Teaching Staff Member assigned to a specific department.
- **Capabilities**:
  - View personal teaching schedule across the week in an organized timetable grid.
  - Inspect vacant periods where the instructor has no scheduled teaching responsibilities.
  - Search and filter genuinely free peer faculty for specific slots, with automatic prioritization of colleagues within the same branch.
  - Create and dispatch substitution requests to eligible colleagues for specific classes and dates.
  - Review incoming substitution requests received from colleagues and explicitly **Accept** or **Reject** them.
  - Cancel pending substitution requests that have not yet been accepted.
  - View personal attendance history (read-only).
  - Submit requests for exam invigilation duties to the HOS.
- **Restrictions**:
  - Cannot edit master class timetables or delete timetable entries.
  - Cannot create other faculty user accounts or modify department settings.
  - Cannot mark or alter faculty attendance records.
  - Cannot approve staged AI timetable uploads.

## 7. SCOPE OF THE PROJECT

### In-Scope (Implemented and Verified Features)
1. **Multi-Tenant Branch Architecture**: Dynamic branch registration (e.g., CME, CSE, EEE, AI_DS) with strict session-based isolation.
2. **Master Timetable Engine**: Weekly matrix rendering (Days: Monday–Saturday; Periods: 1–7) supporting theory lectures, multi-period laboratory blocks, and institutional activity slots (TPC, Library, Sports).
3. **Personal Timetable Dashboard**: Filtered weekly timetable view showing only the authenticated instructor's assigned classes.
4. **Relational Database Integrity**: Fully migrated PostgreSQL schema with primary keys, foreign keys, and unique constraint indexes preventing faculty double-booking (`timetable_no_faculty_double_booking`) and room double-booking (`timetable_no_room_double_booking`).
5. **Daily Attendance Module**: Date-indexed absence logging supporting status flags and optional reasons, automatically excluding absent teachers from availability pools.
6. **Invigilation Module**: Scheduled examination duties tracking (Morning/Afternoon sessions) with mutual exclusion from teaching availability.
7. **Candidate Availability Ranking**: Dynamic candidate discovery that screens out busy, absent, and invigilating teachers, presenting available candidates categorized into "Priority Branch" (same department) and "Other Branches".
8. **Faculty-to-Faculty Substitution Engine**: Full lifecycle tracking (`PENDING`, `ACCEPTED`, `REJECTED`, `CANCELLED`) with zero mutation of original master timetable entries.
9. **AI Timetable Ingestion Gateway**: Server-side integration with Google Gemini multimodal vision (`gemini-2.5-flash`) and n8n webhook automation, generating strict B2.1 JSON contracts.
10. **Staged Timetable Approval Interface**: Interactive review screen allowing the HOS to preview extracted timetables, resolve uncatalogued subjects or teachers, and execute transactional database imports.
11. **Comprehensive Automated Test Coverage**: Verified unit tests, database tests, persistence verification across simulated server restarts, and live end-to-end integration suites.

### Out-of-Scope (Excluded from the Current Implementation)
1. **Automated Algorithmic Timetable Generation**: The system does not generate academic timetables from scratch using genetic algorithms or constraint satisfaction solvers; it manages and ingests institutional schedules.
2. **Automated Substitute Assignment**: The system never assigns a substitute teacher automatically; substitution remains a human decision requiring mutual consent between colleagues.
3. **Direct AI Database Writes**: Gemini AI and n8n pipelines are strictly prohibited from writing directly to live timetable tables; they write solely to temporary staging tables.
4. **External Communication Gateways**: Automated SMS, WhatsApp, or outbound email dispatch systems are not bundled within the core web server.
5. **Native Mobile Applications**: The current software is delivered as a responsive Progressive/Web Application, not a native iOS or Android binary.
6. **Student Attendance & Grading**: Student-facing portals, student attendance marking, and academic gradebook functions are outside the design scope.

## 8. ADVANTAGES

1. **Zero Double-Booking Guarantee**: The combination of real-time application validation and database unique constraints guarantees that neither teachers nor rooms can be assigned to multiple obligations simultaneously.
2. **Rapid Substitution Resolution**: Instructors can identify available colleagues and send substitution invitations in seconds, eliminating manual search overhead.
3. **Cross-Departmental Resource Optimization**: When all departmental colleagues are occupied, the availability engine surfaces qualified, free instructors from allied branches, maximizing institutional teaching capacity.
4. **Preservation of Master Schedule Integrity**: Substitutions are tracked as overlay records; the original master class timetable is never overwritten or corrupted.
5. **Reduced Data Entry Overhead**: With multimodal AI ingestion, an entire semester's class timetable from an image or PDF document can be digitized and verified within minutes.
6. **Robust Data Persistence**: Backed by a cloud PostgreSQL relational database (Neon), ensuring enterprise-grade durability, point-in-time recovery, and zero data loss on application restart.
7. **Clean and Accessible User Interface**: Styled with modern, accessible Vanilla CSS featuring clear contrast ratios, mobile responsiveness, and intuitive status badges.

---

## 9. LIMITATIONS

1. **Dependence on Internet Connectivity**: Because the system relies on cloud-hosted PostgreSQL (Neon) and cloud AI endpoints (Google Gemini), an active internet connection is required for operation.
2. **Multimodal OCR Ambiguity**: While Google Gemini Vision achieves high extraction fidelity, heavily smudged, skewed, low-resolution, or non-standard handwritten timetables may produce parsing discrepancies that require manual correction during the HOS entity resolution review.
3. **Synchronous Class Coverage**: The current substitution model assumes standard 1-to-1 period coverage. It does not support splitting a single class period across multiple substitute teachers.
4. **Session-Based Authentication**: The system currently uses secure server-side session cookies; it does not currently integrate with enterprise single sign-on (SSO) systems such as LDAP, Active Directory, or Google Workspace SAML.

## 10. FUTURE SCOPE

Future enhancements identified for subsequent project versions include:

1. **Automated Push and Messaging Notifications**: Integrating Web Push APIs or SMS/WhatsApp gateway webhooks to notify faculty immediately upon receiving a substitution request.
2. **AI-Powered Substitute Recommendation Scoring**: Developing an intelligent recommendation algorithm that ranks available substitute candidates based on subject domain similarity, historical reciprocation rates, and weekly teaching workload balance.
3. **Advanced Departmental Analytics**: Building visual dashboards to track departmental substitution frequency, leave trends, and teaching hour fulfillment across semesters.
4. **Automated Constraint-Based Timetable Generator**: Expanding the scheduling core to include an automated timetable generator capable of synthesizing conflict-free schedules based on teacher workloads, room capacities, and syllabus hours.
5. **Dedicated Native Mobile App**: Packaging the frontend into native mobile applications using Flutter or React Native for streamlined mobile access and offline timetable caching.

---

## 11. CONCLUSION

The **TecSubstitution** project successfully delivers a reliable, conflict-free, and branch-isolated platform for academic timetable management and faculty substitution in technical institutions. By replacing manual, paper-driven workflows with a centralized digital system, the application eliminates scheduling clashes, optimizes teaching availability, and ensures smooth academic continuity during faculty absences.

The implementation successfully combines robust relational database modeling in PostgreSQL, responsive web design, strict role-based access control, and cutting-edge multimodal AI extraction with mandatory human-in-the-loop administrative approval. Rigorous verification across database schema migrations, persistence recovery tests, and end-to-end operational test suites confirms that the platform is stable, secure, and ready for deployment in technical educational institutions.
